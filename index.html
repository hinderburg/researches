Playable builds of game prototypes.

Colosseum Duel - iteration 5A tactical 1v1 gladiator duel against a bot. Unity, WebGL. HEXBand - prototype 0.21Turn-based hex tactics: paint territory, capture points of interest, attack by moving onto the enemy. Drag or tap controls (switch in the menu). Vs bot or hotseat. HTML/JS. HEXBand - iteration 2 (castles) 0.27Branch iteration2: each player has a castle whose defence grows with the realm and which strikes back; a destroyed warband regroups in it; only fully surrounded areas are taken. Win on territory after round 10 or by storming the enemy castle. HEXBand - iteration 3 (Overlord) 0.42Branch iteration3: an Overlord with a retinue of minions as his shield, sorties by minion type, pits and the road home, a camp to pick minion types and levels. Slay the enemy Overlord or hold more land after 12 rounds. TOADBREW - Alchemical Arena 1.0Tactical 1v1 vs bot: two mechanical frogs each, slingshot jumps across a breakable stone arena, three alchemical flasks on every frog, surface reactions and Link combos (Blast, Meteor, Shatter...). HTML/JS. TOADBREW - iteration 2 (3D arena) 2.15.1Branch iteration2, GDD v2: top-down perspective 3D arena with tall walls to bounce off, pan and zoom, frogs animated part by part, elixir tank colored by element, puddles, Drain Nodes, Reaction Charge, six reactions and six team traits. HTML/JS, Three.js. TOADBREW - iteration 3 (Wild Frog) 3.0.1Branch iteration3: a wild mechanical NPC frog caged on the central column breaks free in round 3, acts first every round with the combo its eyes show; whoever slays it wins. HTML/JS, Three.js.
