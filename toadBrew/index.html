How to play

Goal. Knock out both enemy frogs in one round. After 20 turns each, the side with more HP left wins.

Turns. You and BogWitch take turns. Each turn you have 3 pulls to spend on your frogs any way you like: jumps, dashes and one ability. Tap END to stop early, or spend the whole turn on REST. 15 seconds per turn.

Ultimate. From the 2nd pull your frogs that can jump light up with a golden glow that fades in a few seconds. Jump before it fades out: PERFECT. Two perfect jumps in one turn — the second one is an ULTIMATE LANDING: a shockwave that hits every enemy around, the one you land on takes x1.6. Dashes in water never count, but dash onto a pad and jump from there.

Leapfrog. Jump onto your other frog (or dash to it in water) to ride on its back and jump on from there. The frog below gives the jump a bonus: Bulwark — Springboard (+35% range), Jumper — Relay (free pull), Poison — poisons, Spur — bleeds, Tongue — +25% damage, Mystic — shield.

Jump. Drag back from your frog like a slingshot and release. The dotted arc shows exactly where it lands. You land exactly where you aim. Land on an enemy to hit it and knock it away; the aim shows HIT or NO HIT before you release.

Water. Missed the lily pad? In water you Dash instead (costs 1 Stamina, or HP when Stamina is empty) and take +20% damage. Dash onto a pad to climb out.

Lily pads sink. As soon as a frog lands on a pad it starts sinking, and nothing stops it, even if the frog jumps away (heavy Bulwark makes it faster). It goes under, stays a while and floats up again, but not forever: lotus flowers on a pad show how many times it can still float up. A withered, cracked pad without flowers will sink for good.

Stamina refills by 1 at the start of your turn while on a pad. Dash costs 1, an ability 2 (one per turn). Rest on a pad refills all Stamina and heals 10% HP.

Team bonus. Some pairs have a synergy — check the panel on the team screen.
