How to play

Goal. Knock out both enemy frogs in one round. After 20 turns each, the side with more HP left wins.

Turns. You and BogWitch take turns. On your turn pick one of your frogs and do one action. You have 15 seconds — when time runs out your frog rests.

Jump. Drag back from your frog like a slingshot and release. The dotted arc shows exactly where it lands. Land on an enemy to hit it and knock it back.

Water. Missed the lily pad? In water you Dash instead (costs 1 Stamina, or HP when Stamina is empty) and take +20% damage. Dash onto a pad to climb out.

Lily pads sink while frogs sit on them (heavy Bulwark sinks them twice as fast), stay under water for a while and float up again — but not forever. Lotus flowers on a pad show how many times it can still float up. A withered, cracked pad without flowers will sink for good.

Stamina refills by 1 at the start of your turn while on a pad. Abilities cost 2. Rest on a pad gives +1 more and heals 10% HP.

Team bonus. Some pairs have a synergy — check the panel on the team screen.
