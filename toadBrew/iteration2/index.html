How to play

Goal. Knock out both enemy frogs. 2 vs 2, turn by turn: each frog acts once per round, sides alternate.

Camera. Drag the empty floor to scroll, pinch or use the wheel / ＋－ to zoom.

Jump. Drag back from your frog, aim, release. The arc, bounces, landing ring, knockback and damage show before you let go. Walls bounce you back with a bit of extra range; the glowing Wall Launch Zones add more. The column and crystals block jumps.

Elixir. Each frog carries one element: FIRE damage · ICE shorter next jump · POISON damage over time · LIGHTNING chains to a second enemy.

Reactions. Land on a puddle of another element, or land on your ally to get a Reaction Charge (your element + the ally's) — the next jump fires it anywhere. Steam Veil, Toxic Detonation, Plasma Orb, Toxic Crystal, Static Shell, Neuroshock.

Drain Nodes. Land on a grate under a red pipe: next round a puddle of your element appears nearby. The last frog to land decides the element.

Team trait. Each pair of frogs has its own trait — see the TEAM screen.
